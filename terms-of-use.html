3. AI outputs, safety labels, and accuracy

No independently validated overall accuracy percentage has been established for Embryly. A model’s confidence value, food score, or demonstration result is not a measured probability that a food is safe for you. We do not promise 90%, 100%, or any other numerical accuracy rate. If a legacy screen shows “up to 90%,” it must not be treated as clinical validation or a safety guarantee.

AI may misidentify a dish, ingredient, brand, serving size, cooking method, or nutrient amount. It can generate plausible statements that are wrong, incomplete, inconsistent, or outdated. Barcode records can be missing or refer to a different formulation or country. A photo cannot verify pasteurization, internal cooking temperature, storage history, allergens, cross contact, contamination, freshness, or a recall.

Labels such as Safe, Caution, or Avoid are informational categories, not certification that an actual food is free from risk. Check packaging, preparation, storage, manufacturer information, current official guidance, and your clinician’s instructions. If information conflicts or is missing, do not rely on an app label to resolve the uncertainty.

4. Meal matching and nutritional estimates

Our recommendations select and rank entries in a prepared catalog using information such as pregnancy week, saved ingredient avoidances, dietary and cultural preferences, and recent meal suggestions. AI assists with parts of our content creation and scan analysis; the daily catalog matcher is not a clinician and does not perform a complete nutritional assessment.

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner options are alternative meal ideas, not an instruction to eat every displayed dish or a guarantee of complete daily nutrition. Portions, preparation times, calories, and nutrients are estimates. Ingredient substitutions, brands, cooking losses, and serving sizes affect the result. Catalog recipes have undergone automated editorial checks but are not all kitchen tested or independently clinically approved.

Preferences and allergy tags help filter suggestions but cannot guarantee allergen absence or prevent cross contact. Halal and kosher preferences may surface plant based ideas; certification, sourcing, utensils, and preparation still require your own checks. A lower carbohydrate preference is a relative ranking preference, not a prescribed low carbohydrate diet. Some combinations of restrictions may have limited or no suitable matches. We will not represent availability as proof of suitability.

5. Baby illustrations and food images

Baby and fetus visuals are artistic, AI assisted illustrations organized by pregnancy week, not ultrasound images, diagnostic images, or a depiction of your own baby. Anatomy, proportions, skin appearance, posture, and developmental details may be simplified or inaccurate. They are not to scale. Real pregnancies vary, and a displayed week does not establish gestational age, viability, growth, health, or a due date.

Food images can be generated or illustrative. Appearance does not prove ingredients, portion size, doneness, safety, certification, or nutritional composition. Follow the written ingredient list and preparation guidance, then verify the actual food. Promotional app screens use fictional sample entries; they do not show patient records, measured outcomes, or testimonials.
