Can You Drink Eggnog While Pregnant? Check These Details

Check milk, eggs, alcohol and refrigeration when choosing eggnog during pregnancy, including homemade preparations.

Embryly Editorial Team
Editorial date: September 6, 2026 · Published October 14, 2026 · Sources checked October 14, 2026

A festive mug can hide three separate questions: eggs, milk and alcohol. During pregnancy, choose eggnog made with pasteurized dairy and appropriately treated eggs, without alcohol, and keep it refrigerated as directed. A traditional homemade recipe with uncooked egg is a different choice from a properly processed commercial product. The FDA dairy and egg guide explains the ingredient issue.

Check the carton before the cinnamon

For a commercial drink, read the milk and egg information, alcohol content, storage instructions and opening directions. “Holiday recipe” and “premium” are descriptions, not evidence of pasteurization. Ask the manufacturer if the processing information is unclear.

Some eggnog is sold already mixed with alcohol. Other versions are nonalcoholic until someone adds spirits in the serving bowl. Check both the product and what happened to it at the gathering. A small label beside two separate pitchers is a useful favor to every guest who needs to distinguish them.

Homemade needs a method, not just fresh eggs

Fresh eggs can still contain harmful germs. If a recipe is served without a safe cooking step, use pasteurized eggs or a pasteurized egg product suitable for that preparation. For a cooked eggnog recipe, follow an authoritative method and its temperature instructions rather than improvising based on how thick the mixture looks. FDA holiday food guidance covers raw egg risks in festive recipes.

Adding alcohol is not a substitute for safe preparation. It also does not make the drink appropriate during pregnancy. CDC alcohol guidance recommends avoiding alcohol during pregnancy. Choose a version without it rather than estimating whether a splash is small enough.

The bowl is part of the recipe

Eggnog is perishable. Keep it cold, serve a small amount at a time and return the remaining container to refrigeration. A decorative punch bowl can become a handling problem when the evening runs long. Use appropriate cold holding rather than relying on the fact that the room feels cool.

Do not pour a served portion back into the original carton. Keep cups and ladles clean, and follow the manufacturer's directions after opening. A printed date does not account for a drink left out for hours or mixed with other ingredients.

An easier festive mug

If checking a homemade recipe feels awkward, bring a commercial product with clear processing information or choose another warm drink. You can still enjoy the holiday ritual without trying to reconstruct someone else's kitchen method.

Embryly's barcode scan can help you look up a carton, but it cannot know whether a host added alcohol or how the bowl was held. Compare product information with the label and ask about preparation. Those little details are more useful than a blanket yes or no for every eggnog.
