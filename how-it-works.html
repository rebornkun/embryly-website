How it works

From plate to peace of mind,
in seconds.

Embryly uses AI to help you understand whether a food is a good choice during pregnancy — one scan at a time.

STEP 01

Scan a barcode or snap a photo.

Point your camera at any packaged food's barcode, or take a photo of a meal. Embryly recognizes the food and its ingredients in seconds — no typing, no searching through databases.

App screenshot
STEP 02

Get an instant AI insight.

Our AI analyzes the food against pregnancy nutrition guidance and gives you a clear, color-coded answer: good to go, enjoy in moderation, or better to skip — with a plain-language explanation of why.

App screenshot
STEP 03

Track your journey, week by week.

Log meals, watch key nutrients add up, and follow your baby's growth with weekly milestones. Embryly adapts its guidance as your pregnancy progresses.

App screenshot

A note on accuracy: Embryly's insights are generated by AI and are for general information only. They may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate, and they are not medical advice. Always confirm important dietary decisions with your doctor or midwife.
