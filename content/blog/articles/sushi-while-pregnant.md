The craving is sushi. The confusion is everything on the menu. **During pregnancy, US guidance recommends choosing fully cooked seafood or vegetable rolls and avoiding raw or undercooked fish and shellfish.** The roll name is only a starting point: check the actual filling, toppings and preparation. [FDA pregnancy seafood guidance](source:meat) explains the distinction.

## Start with the ingredients, not the roll name

A California roll may use cooked imitation crab, but that does not make every version identical. A restaurant might add raw fish, roe or a different topping. A tempura roll may have a cooked filling and a raw garnish. Ask what is inside and what goes on top rather than relying on the word “tempura.”

Vegetable rolls are another option, provided the produce is washed and the food is handled appropriately. If a roll contains sprouts, ask for them to be omitted or thoroughly cooked. A plain avocado and cucumber roll is a simpler question than a specialty roll with six ingredients and two sauces.

## A useful order sounds like this

“Could you make a roll with fully cooked shrimp or vegetables, without raw seafood or raw sprouts? Does the sauce contain uncooked egg?” That gives the person preparing your meal something specific to answer.

Ask about separate preparation if raw seafood is handled nearby. This is especially important if you also have an allergy, since “cooked” does not address cross contact. A confident answer about ingredients still cannot guarantee how a kitchen handles every plate. Choose another dish if a key detail cannot be clarified.

## Cooking and mercury answer different questions

Cooking addresses foodborne germs; it does not remove mercury. Identify the fish species as well as whether it is cooked. The [FDA and EPA fish chart](source:fish) helps distinguish lower mercury choices from species to avoid during pregnancy. Salmon and shrimp are examples of lower mercury choices when prepared appropriately. An unidentified tuna filling needs a more specific question.

You do not need to order fish to enjoy a sushi meal. Vegetable rolls, a cooked vegetable side and another protein you tolerate can also make a satisfying combination. Check sauces and any additions just as you would in another cuisine.

## Takeaway sushi needs a plan, too

Collect the meal close to when you intend to eat it. Follow the restaurant's storage instructions and keep perishable food properly chilled during transport. Do not leave a sushi box in a warm car while you run more errands. The [FDA eating-out guide](source:restaurant) includes handling advice that matters after the kitchen finishes its job.

## Where Embryly fits

A food scan can help you identify a dish and organize questions about it. It cannot see every filling or confirm kitchen handling. Use the scan as a prompt to ask about the details that decide your order. If you already ate something and are concerned about an exposure or symptoms, contact your care team for advice specific to the situation.
