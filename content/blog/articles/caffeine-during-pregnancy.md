Your morning coffee is only one line on the caffeine receipt. **ACOG advises limiting caffeine during pregnancy to less than 200 milligrams a day.** Count the total across drinks and other sources rather than deciding by the number of cups. [ACOG's coffee guidance](source:caffeine) is the starting point for this US guide.

## A cup is a container, not a dose

The caffeine in coffee depends on the drink, serving size and preparation. A small espresso drink and a large brewed coffee are not interchangeable. “One coffee” also changes meaning when your mug is much larger than the serving used in a reference chart.

For a packaged drink, read its caffeine information when provided. For a cafe drink, use the chain's current official nutrition information or ask about the specific size and number of shots. Do not use an old screenshot for a new seasonal recipe. If the amount cannot be established, a smaller or decaffeinated option may make planning easier.

## Look beyond coffee

Tea, some soft drinks, energy drinks, chocolate and certain medicines can add caffeine. The point is not to build a perfect spreadsheet for every bite; it is to notice substantial sources that might otherwise disappear from the mental total.

For example, a drink listed as 120 milligrams plus another listed as 60 milligrams adds to 180 milligrams before anything else. Those are arithmetic examples, not estimates for particular products. Use the actual amounts for the items you choose. Ask a pharmacist about medicines rather than changing a prescribed medicine based on a blog.

## Change the routine without losing the ritual

Try a smaller serving, fewer espresso shots, a decaffeinated version or a different drink with a known amount. Decaffeinated does not necessarily mean absolutely caffeine free, so check the information if you need a precise total.

If the comforting part is sitting down with a warm mug, preserve that moment. You can choose the mug and the drink separately. There is no need to treat a lower caffeine choice as a punishment or an elaborate wellness project.

## The general limit is not an individual prescription

Your care team may recommend a different approach based on your health, symptoms or circumstances. Follow that advice. ACOG's [nutrition guide](source:acog) places caffeine within a broader approach to eating during pregnancy; it is one consideration rather than a test of how well you are doing.

## Use Embryly for the food question, then verify the amount

A scan can help identify a packaged beverage or ingredients. It cannot measure caffeine in a cafe's freshly prepared drink from a photograph. Check the size and official product information before counting it. A useful everyday habit is to decide what your first drink contributes, then choose later drinks with that total in mind.
